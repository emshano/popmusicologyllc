Student Music Criticism

On Encouraging Critical Analysis of Popular Music in Undergraduate Students at the University of Pennsylvania

MUSC 1420: Thinking About Popular Music (University of Pennsylvania, 2024–2025)

While teaching Thinking About Popular Music to over 60 UPenn undergraduates across Fall 2024 and Spring 2025, I redesigned unit assignments around Music Notation & Contemporary Popular Music Definitions so students could pair historical readings—from race records and post-WWII pop to 1980s punk, post-soul, and 1990s divas—with their own critical song reviews.

These eleven curated reviews highlight how thoughtfully generalist listeners read popular music, featuring takes on The Waitresses’ “I Know What Boys Like,” Bill Withers’ “Lean On Me,” and Janet Jackson’s “What Have You Done For Me Lately?”, alongside classics by Dionne Warwick, Bessie Smith, Alberta Hunter, The Ronettes, Cameo, and Jimi Hendrix.
